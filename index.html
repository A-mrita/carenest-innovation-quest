STAGE 01

Empathise 💗

Before creating a solution, we needed to understand the people whose everyday lives we wanted to improve.

Getting to know our users

We explored users, their experiences, their needs and the people involved in their care.

Persona Mapping Stakeholder Analysis Journey Mapping
This stage helped us explore the caregiver and patient perspectives, their everyday responsibilities and the challenges surrounding care.
STAGE 02

Define 🎯

Understanding a user is only the beginning. We needed to identify the specific problem that our solution should address.

Finding the real challenge

We explored the problem, identified important pain points and considered the scale of the issue.

Problem Identification Problem Sizing
Our central challenge involved busy caregivers managing the everyday needs of elderly or dependent family members while balancing their own responsibilities.
STAGE 03

Plan & Ideate 💡

One problem can have many possible solutions. We explored different directions before narrowing down our ideas.

From possibilities to one idea

We used creative exercises to generate, connect, compare and prioritise potential solutions.

Brainstorming Mind Mapping Divergent Thinking Convergent Thinking
We explored multiple possibilities and used prioritisation to move towards a solution that could address the caregiving challenge.
STAGE 04

Prototype 🏡

And this is where our idea takes shape. Introducing CareNest — a centralised caregiving companion designed for caregivers and patients.
